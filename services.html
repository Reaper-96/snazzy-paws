Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a grooming session take?

A grooming session generally takes between 2 and 3.5 hours depending on the service and your dog's individual needs.

Are vaccinations required?

Yes. All dogs must have their required vaccinations before receiving grooming services.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes. We welcome dogs of all breeds and sizes.

Can I stay with my dog during grooming?

Owners can stay with their dogs if required. However, dogs are often calmer when their owners are not within sight during grooming.
